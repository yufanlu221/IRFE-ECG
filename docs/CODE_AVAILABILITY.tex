\paragraph{Code availability.}
The implementation, experiment configurations, and instructions required to
reproduce the reported training, routing, ablation, and figure-generation
pipelines for the paper accepted at IEEE BIBM 2026 are available at
\url{https://github.com/yufanlu221/IRFE-ECG}. Original
ECG recordings are not redistributed. Users must obtain the corresponding
records from their public data providers under the applicable licenses and
construct the processed tensors or frozen-feature caches described in the
repository documentation. Pretrained model weights, frozen feature caches,
sample-level predictions, checkpoints, and other large artifacts are not
included. The proposed protocol does not replay raw historical ECG waveforms,
but it retains frozen feature vectors for autonomous source routing and is
therefore raw-ECG-replay-free rather than memory-free.
